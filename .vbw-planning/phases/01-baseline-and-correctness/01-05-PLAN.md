<objective>
Replace the ad-hoc, QueryBuilder-side handling of query operands with a field-owned, operator-aware seam: `cast_query_operand(operator, raw)`. The field decides what an operand means for a given operator; `QueryBuilder` calls the seam once and keeps its SQL-shaping job. On that seam, repair correctness class 5 (write/query casting agreement) and class 6 (strict `:between` shape).

This plan owns `lib/typed_eav/query_builder.rb`, `lib/typed_eav/field/typed_storage.rb`, and the Field files listed in `files_modified` for the whole of wave 5. Phase 2 depends on the query contract being stable afterwards.
</objective>
